ⓘ The Key — what it all means

New here? This is your decoder ring. Every emoji, color, and symbol on the list is explained below — so you always know what we’re telling you about a spot.

How the list works

Same rules as the player lists: spots move up, down, or off entirely. A review drops from Klein or LaBan, a place gets hot, a visit disappoints — the list reacts.

Hype

The third link button next to Menu and the Instagram button — the review, article, or clip that put the spot on the list. A dim button means no link saved yet. (Working name until Matt settles it.)

The Month Lists

Watch 101 September through December — events, openings, debuts, concerts, sports. Still a ranked 101 list at heart, and the 📅 Calendar button in the toolbar flips the same events into a month grid. Dates show single days or runs, plus showtimes when they are set, the Menu button becomes Tickets, and Instagram splits into VENUE and ARTIST buttons (artist optional). A month marked STAGING is still being built.

Geographic Rule

From the 7/29 tape: restaurants stay Philadelphia; bars & breweries deliberately cast a wider net so the ones worth a drive make the cut.

🏷️ Create a Tier

Draws a colored bracket around a contiguous range of players on the current list. Brackets hide automatically when the list is filtered or sorted, and reappear when the list returns to its saved order. Each list keeps its own tiers.
