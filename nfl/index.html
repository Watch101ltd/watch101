Scoring (the FPts Column)

FPts means fantasy points, and every league counts them a little differently. The small gold box in the FPts header switches the whole page between four scoring systems. Click it, pick your league’s style, and every number recalculates on the spot.

STDStandard. No points for catches.
.5 PPRHalf PPR. Half a point per catch. Our default.
PPRFull PPR. A full point per catch.
TE PremTight End Premium. Full PPR, plus an extra half point on every tight end catch.
